A Few Helpful Details

Frequently Asked Questions

A little more clarity before you book your appointment.

How can I book my makeup appointment?
Fill in the short enquiry form or message Isha on WhatsApp. Share your event details and preferred date so availability can be checked.
Do you provide makeup services at the venue?
Studio and at-home or venue enquiries are welcome. Share your event location on WhatsApp to confirm service availability and any applicable travel arrangements.
What is the booking and advance payment process?
A 50% advance is required to confirm a booking. The remaining balance is payable before the service. Booking terms will be shared while confirming your appointment.
Can I reschedule my appointment?
Rescheduling can be discussed and is subject to availability. Please contact Isha as early as possible if your plans change.
